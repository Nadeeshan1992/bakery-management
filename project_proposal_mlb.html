1. Executive Summary

MLB POS System is an end-to-end management solution designed for bakery operations, counter billing, raw ingredient inventory, and customer account management.

Following the successful deployment of Phase 1 (Web POS, Inventory BOM, Customer Profiles, and Executive Reporting), this proposal outlines Phase 2 Technical Expansions requested by MLB Management:

  • SMS Gateway Integration: Automated SMS notifications for receipts, custom cake status updates, and credit period reminders.
  • Product Return & Refund Handling: Complete workflow for customer exchanges, damaged stock classification, and credit notes.
  • MLB Mobile Application (iOS & Android): Mobile app suite featuring Owner Dashboard, Driver Delivery GPS Routing, and Mobile POS.

2. Proposed Technical Modules

Module 1: SMS Gateway Integration

Integrating local Sri Lankan SMS Gateways (Dialog, Mobitel, or Notify.lk API) for automated customer notifications:

  • Order Confirmation SMS: Sends immediate SMS receipt to customers upon checkout containing Order # and Total Amount (Rs.).
  • Custom Order Status Alerts: Automatic SMS when cake status changes (In Production → Ready → Out for Delivery).
  • Credit Payment Reminders: Automated SMS sent 3 days before credit period expiry for wholesale accounts.
  • Promotional Broadcasts: Broadcast festival discount SMS campaigns to registered customer mobile numbers.
Module 2: Product Return & Refund Handling

A structured module to handle returns, replacements, and damaged bakery goods:

  • Customer Return Interface: Search invoice #, select specific items returned, and select reason (Damaged/Expired, Wrong Item, Exchange).
  • Stock Classification: Re-salable items are restocked automatically; damaged items are logged to Spoilage Inventory.
  • Credit Notes & Refunds: Generates printable Credit Notes or processes instant cash/UPI refunds.
Module 3: MLB Mobile Application Suite

Cross-platform mobile applications for iOS & Android built using Flutter / React Native:

  • Owner Dashboard App: Real-time sales ticker, revenue metrics, and push alerts for low stock.
  • Delivery Driver App: Scheduled delivery queue, Google Maps navigation, signature capture, and COD logging.
  • Mobile Tablet POS: Portable ordering interface for staff during peak hours.
